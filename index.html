Osphra: Finding Odor Sources without Mapping Every Point

Independent Research Project | Aug 2026 - Present

Advisor: Prof. Yi Ding, The University of Texas at Dallas

Building a portable electronic nose for real-time volatile compound sensing and odor-source localization without exhaustively mapping an entire space.

  • Paired a BME688 MOX gas sensor with an ESP32-S3 microcontroller.
  • Designed distance-based measurement protocols and custom 3D-printed sensor jigs to standardize airflow and sensor-to-source geometry across trials.
  • Paired the device with an ARCore-tracked smartphone and benchmarked the approach against established gas distribution mapping methods.

MIT EECS & CSAIL

Research Collaborator, Perceptual Engineering | Mar 2025 - Feb 2026

Advisor: Prof. Jas Brooks | Remote

Developed a chemical safety system using electronic tongue technology to detect hazardous interactions between household cleaning products.

  • Built a comprehensive dataset of 44 cleaning products with multiple ion-selective electrode measurements
  • Designed and implemented sensor array circuitry using Arduino-based microcontrollers interfaced with ion-selective electrodes for real-time chemical detection
  • Applied dimensionality reduction techniques (PCA, t-SNE) to visualize chemical signatures and identify discriminative markers despite similar ion signatures across surfactant-based products
